Biography

Junior aka Michael Daniel Duran is my cat. I named him Junior because my two sons are not junior; my sons names are Matthew and Vincent. I also have a daughter named Katelyn. Junior came into my life on March 7th 2025 in the middle of the night when I was working on third shift in southwest Texas. I saw a bunch of kittens by the road. I got out of my work truck and all the kittens scattered but I caught one. Junior finished the shift with me and we went home after work in the morning. Junior is mostly black but has some white spots on him and has short hair. Junior likes buffalo wings and chimichangas but my girlfriend has him only on cat food now. My girlfriend said that Junior need’s structure and discipline. My girlfriend has a water spray bottle for discipline which he hates.

Junior and I moved from southwest Texas shortly after that and now we live near Houston. One time Junior left the apartment and didn’t return for a whole month. At that time, I thought Junior was a goner, I threw away his litter box. The next day Junior came back home and I put an Air Tag on him. He lost two Air Tags in the nearby neighborhood and I went to the backyard of the house with the homeowner but could not find the Air Tags.

One day as we were leaving the apartment we saw Junior inside another apartment in the window and that is when we found out that when Junior goes outside, he stays with other families some times.

We recently got another cat named Felix and now they are best friends and Junior doesn’t leave the apartment anymore. Felix was adopted from the local Petco store; he is mostly white and has some grey spots on him. When Junior gets sick, I take him to the Vets at the Petco where Felix was adopted at and they charge me a pretty penny. Hopefully Junior lives a long and healthy life. I told my girlfriend when Junior dies of old age we are going to take him to the taxidermy but she hates that idea, I have about 20 years to convince her.
